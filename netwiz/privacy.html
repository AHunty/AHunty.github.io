Netwiz Privacy Policy

Netwiz IP calculator for iPhone · Last updated 7 November 2026

Netwiz is an IP and subnet calculator with network tools. Everything it works out stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, receive or share any of your data.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your calculation tape (recent calculations) and settings

Permissions

  • Local network lets Netwiz ping, trace and check ports on devices on your network, such as your router, and list the devices it finds. This only happens when you ask.
  • Location is used only so iOS will tell Netwiz the name of the Wi-Fi network you're connected to. Netwiz doesn't record or store where you are.

You can turn either off in iPhone Settings. The calculators keep working without them.

Network tools

Ping, traceroute and port checks go only to the address you enter. Netwiz has no server of its own, and nothing about your network is sent to us.

Exports

Anything you export or copy (PDF, text) goes only where you send it, using the iPhone share sheet.

No tracking

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
