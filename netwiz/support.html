Quick answers

  • Entering an address: spin the wheels, one per octet plus the CIDR wheel, or switch to Typing. Results update as soon as the wheel stops.
  • IPv6: choose IPv6 in the Calculator tab's pill.
  • Splitting a network: the VLSM tab sizes subnets from the number of hosts each one needs.
  • Hex and binary: the Hex tab converts between bases and shows every bit of an IPv4 address.
  • Your network: the Network tab shows your Wi-Fi and IP details, and does ping, traceroute, port checks and a device scan. Allow Local Network access when asked, or turn it on in Settings > Netwiz.
  • Wi-Fi name not showing? iOS only gives apps the Wi-Fi name when Location is allowed. Turn it on in Settings > Netwiz > Location.
  • Saving your work: past calculations are on the tape. Use Export to share them as a PDF or text.
