Shot Director by Hunty

Questions, problems or ideas? Email ashunty@gmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Quick answers

  • Timing a take: tap ACTION to start and CUT to stop, then tap Good, Bad, Incomplete or Best. Tap the clapper slate to skip it.
  • Hit CUT by mistake? Tap Resume within a few seconds to carry on the same take.
  • Exporting your shot log: tap the share button (top right) and choose Excel, PDF, CSV or text. "Best takes only" exports just your circled takes.
  • Several shoots: tap the Production bar to start a new production or switch between them.
  • Clapper sound and torch flash: turn them on or off in Setup > Digital Clapper.
  • Apple Watch: open Shot Director on your iPhone first, then on your watch. ACTION, CUT and ratings stay in sync.
