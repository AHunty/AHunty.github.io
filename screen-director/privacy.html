Screen Director Privacy Policy

Screen Director for Mac and SD Remote for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 10 November 2026

Screen Director shows song words, Bible readings, slides and videos in church. Your songs, services and settings stay on your Mac. We don't collect, receive or share any of your data.

What the app stores on your Mac

  • Your songs, service plans, notices, timers, settings and imported slides
  • A daily backup of these, kept for 30 days
  • A list of which songs were shown and when, so you can do your CCLI copy report

Permissions

  • Local network lets Screen Director send slides over NDI to video equipment, and lets SD Remote on your iPhone or iPad control it. Remote control needs the 4-digit PIN shown on your Mac, and only works on your own network.
  • Controlling Keynote is used only to turn PowerPoint and Keynote files into slides, when you import them.

The internet

  • Screen Director checks this website now and then for a newer version. Only the version number is downloaded; nothing about you or your church is sent.
  • Websites you add to a service, and pages you import song words from, are loaded only when you ask.

No tracking

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
