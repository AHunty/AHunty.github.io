Screen Director Support

Free worship presentation software for Mac, with SD Remote for iPhone and iPad

Questions, problems or ideas? Email ashunty@gmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Download

Download Screen Director for Mac (macOS 15 or later). Open the file and drag Screen Director to Applications.

Quick answers

  • Your first service: Service tab > New Service > Divine Service. Add hymns from the Songs tab and readings from the Bible tab, then click Show Output.
  • PowerPoint and Keynote: drag them onto the Slides tab. Screen Director uses Keynote (free from Apple) in the background to turn them into slides. Click OK the first time your Mac asks if Screen Director may control Keynote.
  • Practising: click Rehearse. The projector and livestream stay as they are while you try things.
  • Stage display: Timers tab > Stage Display. Pick a layout, or make your own with New Layout.
  • Livestream: Video Outputs sends full-screen slides and a see-through lower third over NDI to your video switcher or streaming software.
  • iPhone and iPad remote: install SD Remote, join the same Wi-Fi, and type the PIN shown in Screen Director > Settings > Remote.
  • Stream Deck and MIDI: Settings > Remote lists the web addresses and MIDI notes for each button.
  • Backups: a copy of your songs, services and settings is made every day. Settings > Safety restores one.
