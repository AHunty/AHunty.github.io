TimeCruiser Privacy Policy

TimeCruiser app for iPhone · Last updated 1 November 2026

TimeCruiser logs your work time, kilometres and receipts and builds claim reports. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't collect, receive or share any of it.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your activities, times, job notes, trips and kilometres
  • Receipt photos and the details read from them (read on your iPhone, never uploaded)
  • Your settings, such as your home and work addresses, company name and logo

Permissions

  • Location measures the kilometres you drive while a travel activity runs, and records where a work day starts
  • Motion notices when you start driving so the app can ask whether to log the trip
  • Camera and photos let you photograph receipts or pick a logo
  • Notifications remind you about running activities and trips

You can turn any of these off in iPhone Settings. The app keeps working, just without that feature.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you switch on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't see it.

Exports

Reports you export (PDF, Excel, CSV) go only where you send them, using the iPhone share sheet.

No tracking

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
