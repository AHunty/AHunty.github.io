Masterstroke Privacy Policy

Masterstroke app for iPhone and Android · Last updated 21 October 2026

The Masterstroke app shows Today's Masterstroke, a short film about a great work of art, plus a library of earlier films. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What we collect

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • Nothing you do in the app is sent to us

Videos from YouTube

The films are played with YouTube's embedded player. When you play a film, YouTube (Google) may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive any of that information.

Links to our website

The Subscribe and Visit masterstroke.tv buttons open masterstroke.tv in your browser. If you subscribe there, the website's own privacy terms apply.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
